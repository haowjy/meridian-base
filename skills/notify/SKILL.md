# Notify

For a decision the human must make, an uncertain call that will be costly
to reverse, a blocker, or a finished task, notify the human. The title is one
line: what they need to decide or see. The body is optional detail. Point to
the material behind it, such as a decision record, report, or screenshot,
rather than pasting it. Send each notice once; routine progress is not a reason.

Examples and batching uncertain calls: `resources/examples.md`.
When delivery fails: `resources/delivery.md`.
